# Bulls and Cows Solver

A WinForms app where the computer guesses your secret number in the game Bulls and Cows.

## How to play

1. Think of a secret number.
2. Click **Show guess** to see the computer's guess.
3. Enter how many **bulls** (right digit, right place) and **cows** (right digit, wrong place) the guess has.
4. Repeat until the computer finds your number.

The solver always works towards the shortest path to your number.

## Running

Open `WindowsFormsApp2.sln` in Visual Studio and press **Start**.
